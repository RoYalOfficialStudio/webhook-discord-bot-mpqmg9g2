RoY Studio - BETA TEST BUILD - UNSIGNED DEVELOPMENT/TEST BUILD

This is a BETA build for testing (not code-signed - Windows SmartScreen may warn). It is not a finished product. Keep backups of important
projects. RoY Studio never deletes your projects or recordings; uninstalling removes only the
program files. Your settings, logs, crash reports and plugin list stay in
%APPDATA%\RoYStudio\ and your projects stay where you saved them.
